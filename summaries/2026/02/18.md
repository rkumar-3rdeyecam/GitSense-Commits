6.  **CSV Download Workflow**: A dedicated sub-workflow is established for generating and consolidating coaching CSV files, spanning multiple Lambda functions and involving S3 for temporary and final storage, and presigned URLs for downloads.
7.  **Event-Driven Communication**: The use of EventBridge in `CoachingBackend.ts` to publish `Coaching completion date update` events points to an event-driven architectural pattern for certain business logic.
