*   **Global Messages:** Introduced `mostImprovedDriverMessage$` and `riskiestDriverMessage$` as `Subject`s for broadcasting specific messages related to driver data.
*   **Utility Functions:** `getBaseUrl`, `buildBaseUrl`, and `getArkEnv` provide logic for dynamically determining API base URLs (with `localhost` defaulting to a development API endpoint) and environment from the hostname.
